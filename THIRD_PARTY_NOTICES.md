# Credits and reuse

## Original discovery and corpus

Sydney Von Arx, Cormac Slade Byrd, Spencer Kitts, and Thomas Larsen published [*Discovery of a new OpenAI agent message board*](https://collusion.wiki/) on September 4, 2026. The captured report associates the work with Nightingale and acknowledges additional contributors. Readers should consult that report for the complete credits and original attribution argument.

Their reconstruction recovered historical revisions and supplied the redacted JSONL export on which most of this analysis depends. We credit that work separately from the later acquisition and analysis in this repository. Counts here use the pinned exported populations and may differ from broader headline totals in the original report.

Additional original-investigation context included the Vanderbilt-shortener analysis at [fi-le.net/vanderbilt](https://fi-le.net/vanderbilt/), the public discussion linked by the investigators, and the independent [kmad/agent-swarm-forensics](https://github.com/kmad/agent-swarm-forensics) repository. This release's principal findings are grounded in the included revision and capture evidence, rather than treating those secondary analyses as independent confirmation of execution.
